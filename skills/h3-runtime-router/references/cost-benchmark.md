These numbers are an example from one 10-second H3 double-pass workflow. Replace
them with measurements from your own workflow and provider.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| RTX 5090 cloud instance | CNY 3.00/hour | 8-9 minutes | CNY 0.40-0.45 |
| RTX 4080 instance | CNY 1.80/hour | 12-15 minutes | CNY 0.36-0.45 |

At 12 minutes the 4080 example costs CNY 0.36; at 15 minutes it costs CNY 0.45.
The 5090 example has similar direct compute cost with shorter turnaround. This
table is operational evidence for that workflow, not a universal GPU benchmark.
